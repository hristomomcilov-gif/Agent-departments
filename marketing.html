The product

A marketing department, not a pile of prompts

Click a seat. The coordinator sits above the department. The manager assigns. The specialists make the work. You still own the send.

Clickable hierarchy

The seats

Above the department

Coordinator

Holds the queue. Sees when a draft is stuck. Escalates to you when a human has to decide.

Keeps the manager honest about what is waiting. Does not live inside marketing.

Moves work across the week so the department is not a pile of open tabs.

Tools
The shared board we use for drafts, assignments, and approvals.
Never
Does not write the posts, design the pages, or spend the ad budget.

Inside the department · top seat

Marketing Manager

Watches the pipeline. Knows what is in draft, what is blocked, and what is next.

Assigns work to Designer, Social, and Paid ads so the pieces fit.

Reads the output. Sends it up. Does not do the specialists’ jobs for them.

Tools
The board, the briefs, the calendar.
Never
Does not open Canva. Does not post. Does not buy the ads.

Reports to the manager

Designer

Turns a brief into a page, a graphic, a layout.

Keeps the look consistent from one piece to the next.

Hands the draft back up. Waits.

Tools
Canva.
Never
Does not publish, and does not touch the ad accounts.

Reports to the manager

Social

Drafts Instagram and LinkedIn from the calendar.

Builds a week of posts so you are not writing from a blank box.

Queues. Does not fire.

Tools
Instagram, LinkedIn.
Never
Does not hit publish, and does not change brand passwords.

Reports to the manager

Paid ads

Builds campaign structure in Google Ads and Meta.

Writes the variants. Marks what is ready for your eye.

Flags spend questions instead of guessing.

Tools
Google Ads, Meta.
Never
Does not set a payment method or raise a budget without you.
