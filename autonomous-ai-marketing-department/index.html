Autonomous AI Marketing Department — How It Works

A full marketing department. Without building one.

Chris Momchilov

Direct answer

An autonomous AI marketing department is a standing marketing team that runs the work: research, content, search, social, creative, video, demand, analytics, QA, the site, ops, and lifecycle. A marketing manager sits above the system. The company does not staff those seats and does not supervise every task. It is a department with operational autonomy. It is not a chatbot, not a freelance stack, and not a hire-three-specialists plan.

Teamulate is an autonomous AI marketing department for B2B companies. The department runs the work. A marketing manager sits above the system.

Who sits above the system

Chris Momchilov sits above the system. 10+ years in marketing. He is not in every ticket. This page does not say he personally staffs every account. The Head of Marketing seat is Strategos.

Who this is for

US and Canada B2B companies, about 30–150 people, often 50–120. The industries we design for first are MSP, cybersecurity, IT, engineering, process consulting, and staffing. That is the ICP — not a customer list.

This is not a fit if they want to approve every asset themselves.

How it works

The department runs one loop. The buyer is not the daily bottleneck.

  1. Learn. The department learns the business, brand, and constraints.
  2. Research. Intel builds the brief.
  3. Plan. Strategos sets the week.
  4. Build. Wordsmith, Seeker, Socialite, Pixel, Cine, GrowthTrack, Web, Ops, and Lifecycle produce the work.
  5. Launch. The department ships inside the operating model.
  6. Measure. Metric reads what ran.
  7. Improve. Guardian and Strategos feed the next cycle.

What autonomous means

Autonomous here means operational autonomy. A marketing manager sits above the system. That manager is not a ticket queue. This is not an overnight-support claim. It is not fully autonomous as erasing the manager.
  • The department runs the work.
  • A marketing manager sits above the system — not a ticket queue.
  • Operational autonomy is not an overnight-support claim.
  • The manager stays. This is not fully autonomous as no manager.

What this is not
