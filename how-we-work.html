The first week

Roles. Access. First tasks. Then the loop.

This is not a feature list. It is what the week actually looks like when we stand the department up.

  1. Day 01

    Roles

    We name the seats before anyone works. Coordinator above. Marketing Manager. Designer. Social. Paid ads. You above all of that. If a seat is unclear, we do not start.

  2. Day 02

    Access

    The agents get the tools the job requires. You keep the passwords. You decide what they can see. I set the permissions. Nobody invents a login.

  3. Day 03

    First tasks

    The manager assigns real work: a page, a post, an ads skeleton. You see drafts. Nothing is sent.

  4. Then

    Routines

    Daily and weekly loops. The coordinator watches the queue. The manager assigns. The specialists execute. You stay the send gate.

The send gate

Sending, passwords, and money stay with you. I am not a second owner. I built the department so that is still true at eleven at night.
