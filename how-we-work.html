How it works

Learn. Research. Plan. Build. Launch. Measure. Improve.

A full marketing department. Without building one.

Capacity and visibility first. The seats draft inside guardrails. You stay on spend, send, and publish. Built on Grok Bot — your accounts, your tools.

The cycle

  1. 1 · Learn

    We learn the business, brand, and constraints before anyone drafts. If a seat is unclear, we do not start.

  2. 2 · Research

    Intelligence maps audience, market, and gaps. This is input for the plan — not a launch.

  3. 3 · Plan

    Head of Marketing sets priorities, the calendar, and the rails the other seats work inside.

  4. 4 · Build

    Content, Creative Studio, Search / GEO, Demand, Web / CRO, Lifecycle, and Ops draft the work. QA reads it before it reaches you.

  5. 5 · Launch

    You approve spend, send, and publish. Nothing fires on its own. You keep the passwords and the payments.

  6. 6 · Measure

    Analytics reports what your own tools recorded. We do not invent lift or promise ROI.

  7. 7 · Improve

    The next cycle uses what we can see. Scope stays inside the plan you bought — Core, Growth, or Scale.

The first week

  1. Day 01

    Roles

    We name the seats before anyone works: Head of Marketing through QA. You sit above all of that.

  2. Day 02

    Access

    Seats get the tools the job requires. You keep the passwords. You decide what they can see. Nobody invents a login.

  3. Day 03

    First tasks

    Real work is assigned: a page, a post, a campaign skeleton. You see drafts. You keep the wheel.

  4. Then

    Routines

    Daily and weekly loops. Head of Marketing assigns. Specialists execute. QA checks. You approve the gates.

You keep the wheel

AWS, Grok Bot, martech, and ad spend are client-owned and billed separately. Passwords, payments, and the direction stay with you.
