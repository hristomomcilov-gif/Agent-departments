AI marketing automation vs AI agents

A full marketing department. Without building one.

Chris Momchilov, Teamulate, Barrie, Ontario

Direct answer

Marketing automation runs rules and zaps on a path someone already designed. AI agents, in this model, are named seats that draft new work — pages, posts, briefs, sequences — so the company does not have to staff those seats. Teamulate is an autonomous AI marketing department. It is not a zap tool and it does not replace an ESP. The beachhead is the department. This page only explains the contrast.

Teamulate is an autonomous AI marketing department for B2B companies. The department runs the work. Strategos is Head of Marketing. Chris Momchilov is the founder above Teamulate.

See the team in action See how it works

What this is

  • Automation: journeys, triggers, and zaps on a path already approved.
  • Named seats (11 only): Strategos, Scout, Wordsmith, Seeker, GrowthTrack, Pixel, Flow, Socialite, Nexus, Metric, Guardian. They draft new work.
  • Head: Strategos orchestrates. A client marketing manager is not required.
  • Founder: Chris Momchilov sits above Teamulate. 10+ years in marketing. He is not a hired client manager.

What this is not

  • Not a replacement for HubSpot, Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or any ESP.
  • Not a zap builder.
  • Not the beachhead page.
  • Not a requirement that the client hire or sit as marketing manager.
  • Not a sixty-item workflow index. Named titles are the eighteen on /preview/workflows/.

How the contrast lands

Model Does Limit
Marketing automation Runs a path already designed Misses new briefs, pages, and campaigns when nobody is producing them
A pile of unnamed agents Isolated tasks Misses a Head of Marketing and a named roster
Teamulate A department that drafts new work in tools you already pay for Does not replace your ESP or become your zap tool

Who is above the work

Strategos is Head of Marketing and orchestrates the eleven seats. Chris Momchilov is the founder above Teamulate. The department runs the work. The client is not the daily manager.

Who this is for

US and Canada B2B, about 30–150 people, often 50–120, in MSP, cyber, IT, engineering, process consulting, and staffing. Locked ICP. Not a customer list. Useful if the pinch is “we have HubSpot and still no queue.” Not useful if the ask is “build us more zaps.”
