AI agents for marketing

Direct answer

An AI marketing agent is a seat with one job — brief, draft, cut, or check — that runs inside rules you set. Agents do not replace the yes on spend, send, or publish. Teamulate uses named seats (Intelligence, Content, Search / GEO, and others) rather than an advertised swarm. We do not publish an agent count.
