How it works

Learn. Research. Plan. Build. Launch. Measure. Improve.

Specialized agents run the work. Chris Momchilov sets strategy and guardrails. The department publishes, launches, and spends inside that plan.

The seven steps

  1. 1 · Learn

    We learn the business, brand, and constraints before anyone drafts. If a seat is unclear, we do not start.

  2. 2 · Research

    Intelligence maps audience, market, and gaps. This is input for the plan — not a launch.

  3. 3 · Plan

    Head of Marketing sets priorities, the calendar, and the rails the other seats work inside.

  4. 4 · Build

    Specialists build the work. Guardian checks quality and brand as it ships.

  5. 5 · Launch

    The department publishes, launches, and spends across the channels in the strategy.

  6. 6 · Measure

    Analytics reports what your own tools recorded. We do not invent lift or promise ROI.

  7. 7 · Improve

    The next cycle uses what we can see. Then the loop starts again.
