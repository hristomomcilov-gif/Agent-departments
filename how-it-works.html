How it works

Learn through experience

Specialized agents run the work. Chris Momchilov sits above the system. Seven seats of the work — not a guaranteed loop, and not a promise of ROI.

  1. Launch

    Stand up the department around your brand and channels. If a seat is unclear, we do not start.

  2. Research

    Intel maps audience, market, and gaps. This is input for the plan — not a launch.

  3. Ideas

    Strategos sets the angles, priorities, and rails the other seats work inside.

  4. Scale

    Add seats, integrations, and workflows as the plan grows. Built to expand, not to invent volume.

  5. Content

    Wordsmith, Pixel, Cine, and Web draft inside those rails. Guardian checks quality as it ships.

  6. Optimize

    The next pass uses what we can see. We do not invent lift.

  7. Analytics

    Metric reports what your own tools recorded. Empty is allowed. No promised ROI.
