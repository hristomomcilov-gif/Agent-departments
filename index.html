Marketing loop

Learn through Improve

The same cycle, in crawlable HTML. Routine work stays in the rails. Launch waits on you.

  1. Learn

    Business, brand, and constraints before anyone drafts.

  2. Research

    Audience, market, and the brief behind the next asset.

  3. Plan

    Head of Marketing sets priorities and the rails.

  4. Build

    Specialist seats draft inside those rails.

  5. Launch

    You approve spend, send, and publish.

  6. Measure

    Analytics reports what your tools recorded.

  7. Improve

    The next pass uses what we can see.
