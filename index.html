How it works

Seven steps. You stay on the gates.

Routine work stays in guardrails. Spend, send, and publish wait for a human yes. You keep the passwords and the payments.

  1. 1

    Learn

    We learn the business, brand, and constraints before seats start drafting.

  2. 2

    Research

    Intelligence maps audience, market, and gaps you can actually use.

  3. 3

    Plan

    Head of Marketing sets priorities, the calendar, and the rails.

  4. 4

    Build

    Specialist seats draft the assets inside those rails.

  5. 5

    Launch

    You approve spend, send, and publish. Nothing fires on its own.

  6. 6

    Measure

    Analytics reports what happened — from your tools, in one view.

  7. 7

    Improve

    The next cycle uses what we can see, not a promised lift.
