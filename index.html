The skeleton

Not the whole company. Marketing, as it actually sits: you, a coordinator, a manager, then the people who make the work.

Three things that are true

  1. Cheaper than hiring a team

    You are not putting a designer, a social person, and a media buyer on payroll. You are standing up a department I cut and you run.

  2. Work continues while you sleep

    Routines keep drafting. The queue fills. In the morning you have work to approve, not a blank page.

  3. You are the only one who says “send”

    Outbound, passwords, payments. If a human has to decide, that human is you. The agents stop at the gate.

What I actually do

I do not sit in the seats. I build the seats, then I stay the person who designed them.

  • Create the agents.
  • Cut their roles so they do not step on each other.
  • Give them the tool access the job requires.
  • Train them on the tasks.
  • Set the routines and automations.
  • Pull you in only for the decisions that are yours: passwords, payments, and anything that goes out the door.
